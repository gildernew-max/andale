Privacy — Ándale

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Ándale is español mexicano real, for people past the basics. This page covers the web app at https://gildernew-max.github.io/andale/ and, later, the TestFlight build. It is not a store listing.

What stays on your device

There is no account, no sign-in, and no email gate.

Progress lives on your device: XP, streak, gems, hearts, lessons finished, flashcards, theme, Español/English, the name you type at the door if you type one, and which voice you picked. That is stored locally as andale-v3. Clear site data or delete the app and it is gone. We do not keep a copy.

Readings use the voices already on your device. Paulina is the default when your phone or computer has her. We do not record you and we do not upload audio.

What we do not collect

We do not run ads. We do not sell data. We do not use analytics in the app. We do not ask for location, camera, or microphone.

Who else might see something

The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary hosting logs (time, IP, page). That is their service, not a profile we build.

If you install a TestFlight build, Apple’s TestFlight rules apply.

Kids

Ándale is for people past the basics. It is not directed at children under 13.
