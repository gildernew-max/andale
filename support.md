## Help

If something breaks on the web app, hard-refresh the page. Progress lives on your device. If you clear site data, the path resets.

For the iOS app: update to the latest build, force-quit, and reopen. Subscriptions and restores go through your Apple ID (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or the App Store account page).
