Privacy — Ándale

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Ándale is español mexicano real, for people past the basics. This page covers the web app at https://gildernew-max.github.io/andale/ and the iOS app when it is on the App Store or TestFlight.

What stays on your device

There is no Ándale account and no email gate to use the app.

Progress lives on your device: XP, streak, gems, hearts, lessons finished, flashcards, theme, Español/English, the name you type at the door if you type one, and which voice you picked. That is stored locally (for example as andale-v3). Clear site data or delete the app and it is gone. We keep only your email, if you leave it, and anonymous usage events, as explained in "Your email and your data."

Readings use the voices already on your device. We do not record you and we do not upload audio.

What we collect and what we don't

We do not run ads. We do not sell data. We don't use outside analytics services or trackers. We keep anonymous usage events ourselves, in our own Google Sheet. We do not ask for location, camera, or microphone access for learning features.

Purchases (if any) go through Apple. Apple handles payment data under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Who else might see something

The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary hosting logs (time, IP, page). That is their service, not a profile we build. If you leave your email, or when the app records usage events, that data is stored with Google in our Google Sheet, under Google's privacy policy.

If you use TestFlight or the App Store build, Apple’s rules for those services also apply.

Kids

Ándale is for people past the basics. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change.

Tu correo y tus datos

Si nos dejas tu correo, guardamos tu dirección, el idioma que usas y la fecha. Solo lo usamos para avisarte sobre Ándale, como cuando hay historias nuevas. Nunca vendemos ni compartimos tu correo.

También registramos cómo se usa la app (por ejemplo, si abres o terminas una historia) con un identificador anónimo del dispositivo, nunca con tu correo. Lo usamos para mejorar Ándale.

Estos datos se guardan en Google Sheets, en nuestra cuenta de Google, a la que solo nosotros tenemos acceso.

Para borrar tu correo o dejar de recibir avisos, escríbenos a gildernew@gmail.com y lo eliminamos.

Your email and your data

If you leave your email, we save your address, the language you use, and the date. We only use it to tell you about Ándale, like when new stories are out. We never sell or share your email.

We also record how the app is used (for example, whether you open or finish a story) with an anonymous device ID, never with your email. We use this to make Ándale better.

This data is stored in Google Sheets, in our Google account, which only we can access.

To delete your email or stop getting updates, write to us at gildernew@gmail.com and we'll remove it.
