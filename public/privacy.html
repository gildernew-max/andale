Privacy — Ándale

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Última actualización: 13 de octubre de 2026

Ándale is español mexicano real, for people past the basics. This page covers the web app at https://gildernew-max.github.io/andale/ and the iOS app when it is on the App Store or TestFlight.

What stays on your device

There is no Ándale account. You can use the app without giving your email. Reserving is optional.

No hay cuenta de Ándale. Puedes usar la app sin dar tu correo. Reservar es opcional.

Progress lives on your device: XP, streak, gems, hearts, lessons finished, flashcards, theme, Español/English, the name you type at the door if you type one, and which voice you picked. That is stored locally (for example as andale-v3). Clear site data or delete the app and it is gone. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

Readings use the voices already on your device. We do not record you and we do not upload audio.

If you reserve founding annual, the email you type is saved only on this device. It never reaches us, and we don't share it.

Si reservas el plan anual de fundador, el correo que escribes se guarda solo en este dispositivo. No nos llega ni lo compartimos.

The app sends us a few anonymous signals: when you open it, when you finish a scene, when you see the plan screen, and when you tap the annual plan. Each one carries only the date and a random number made on your device. They never include your name, your email, your recordings, or what you read or say.

La app nos envía unas pocas señales anónimas: cuando la abres, cuando terminas una escena, cuando ves la pantalla del plan y cuando tocas el plan anual. Cada una lleva solo la fecha y un número al azar creado en tu dispositivo. Nunca incluyen tu nombre, tu correo, tus grabaciones ni lo que lees o dices.

What we do not collect

We do not run ads. We do not sell data. We do not use third-party analytics in the app. We do not ask for location, camera, or microphone access for learning features.

Purchases (if any) go through Apple. Apple handles payment data under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Who else might see something

The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary hosting logs (time, IP, page). That is their service, not a profile we build.

If you use TestFlight or the App Store build, Apple’s rules for those services also apply.

Kids

Ándale is for people past the basics. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change.

What we count / Qué contamos

We count which steps you reach (like finishing a lesson or seeing a plan) to improve Ándale. It's anonymous: a random ID for this device, and we don't store your name, email or IP address.

Contamos a qué pasos llegas (como terminar una lección o ver un plan) para mejorar Ándale. Es anónimo: un ID aleatorio de este dispositivo, y no guardamos tu nombre, correo ni dirección IP.
